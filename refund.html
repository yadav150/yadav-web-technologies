Refund Policy

Please review this policy before starting a project or making a payment.

No Refunds

Payments made for services, development work, maintenance, bug fixing, customisation or other project-related work are non-refundable.

1. Payment Is Non-Refundable

Once payment has been made for an accepted service or project, the payment is non-refundable.

This applies whether the payment is made before development starts, during development, or after a stage of the work has been completed.

2. Developer Fixes Are Allowed

No-refund does not mean that a valid project issue is ignored.

If a delivered website, application or system has a genuine bug or implementation issue related to the agreed project scope, the developer will review and fix the issue according to the project conditions.

3. Corrections Within Agreed Scope

Corrections related to the originally agreed requirements can be handled by the developer as part of the applicable project support or review process.

4. New Features Are Not Fixes

A request for a new feature, new page, additional module, redesign, new workflow or functionality that was not part of the agreed scope is considered additional work.

Additional work may require a separate charge.

5. Client-Side Changes

If the website or application is modified by the client or another developer after delivery, resulting issues may require separate assessment and work.

6. Third-Party Services

Payments made to external providers such as hosting companies, domain providers, APIs, subscriptions or other third-party platforms are subject to their own policies.

7. Project Cancellation

If a client decides not to continue with a project after payment, the amount already paid remains non-refundable.

Any completed project work and supplied deliverables will be considered according to the agreed project scope.

8. Maintenance and Bug-Fixing Services

Payments for completed maintenance, correction or bug-fixing work are non-refundable.

Where an issue falls within the accepted work scope, the developer may correct the issue instead of providing a refund.

9. Project-Specific Conditions

A specific project quotation or written project arrangement may provide additional information about the applicable correction, support or delivery process.
