About Us

Building the future of web from Assam.

Yadav Web Technologies is a web-building software and technology company founded in 2026, providing modern, affordable, and reliable digital solutions for businesses, organizations, associations, student unions, NGOs, educational institutions, and individuals.

Our Story

A company built on real experience

From small utility projects to full-scale digital platforms — our work is driven by a simple belief: technology should work for everyone.

Yadav Web Technologies was founded in 2026 with a clear mission — to make modern, professional, and affordable digital solutions accessible to organizations of every size. From small associations and student unions to enterprises and educational institutions, we build websites, software, and automation systems that solve real problems.

We specialize in web development, custom software solutions, digital management systems, secure authentication, backend integration, and business automation. Our work is guided by a commitment to quality, clarity, and long-term reliability — not just quick delivery.

Every project we take on is treated as a partnership. We understand that behind every website or system is a real organization with real people depending on it to work every day. That responsibility shapes how we build, test, and deliver.

His work spans frontend development, backend integration, business automation, and secure authentication systems — driven by a belief that great technology should be powerful, affordable, and easy to use.
