Website & Software Development Pricing

Pricing is based on the actual project scope, functionality, design requirements, integrations and level of customization required.

What Affects Pricing

Project scope determines the final development cost.

The following factors can significantly change the amount of work required for a project.

Number of Pages

More pages and content structures generally require additional design and implementation.

Functionality

Login systems, dashboards, forms, workflows, search, filters and application logic affect development scope.

Database & Data Management

Projects requiring structured records, permissions and data operations involve additional implementation.

Integrations

Firebase, APIs, authentication services, third-party platforms and other integrations can affect development complexity.

Design Requirements

Custom interface design, responsive behaviour, reusable components and specialized layouts can change project scope.

Ongoing Requirements

Future updates, additional modules, maintenance and feature expansion can be discussed separately according to need.

Questions about project pricing?

Can additional features be discussed later?

Yes. Additional functionality can be reviewed separately according to project scope and future requirements.
