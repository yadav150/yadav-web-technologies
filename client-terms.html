Client Services

Client Service Terms

These terms explain how website, application, software, ERP, custom tool and maintenance projects are handled by Yadav Web Technologies.

Last updated: 2026

These terms are intended to keep project requirements, pricing, communication, delivery and ongoing support clear for both sides.

1. Project Requirements

Work is carried out according to the requirements discussed and accepted for the project.

Features, pages, modules, workflows, integrations and other requirements should be communicated as clearly as possible before development begins.

2. Project Scope

The quoted price applies to the agreed project scope.

Requests for additional pages, features, modules, workflows, integrations or major changes may require additional work and additional charges.

3. Pricing

Prices shown on the website are category-based starting prices unless a specific quotation has been provided.

The final project price depends on the actual scope, complexity, features and technical requirements.

4. Payment

Payment arrangements will be communicated before the project is started.

Depending on the project, development may begin after an agreed initial payment or confirmation.

Any remaining payment arrangement will be discussed according to the project scope and delivery plan.

5. Client-Provided Information

The client is responsible for providing the content, information, images, logos, documents and other materials required for the project when applicable.

Delays in providing required material may affect the project schedule.

6. Development and Changes

Development is carried out according to the agreed scope.

Major changes after development has started may affect delivery time and project cost.

Small corrections related to the agreed requirement may be handled as part of the normal project review.

7. Delivery

The project is delivered after the agreed work has been completed and reviewed.

Actual delivery time depends on the project size, feedback, content availability and technical requirements.

8. Review and Corrections

The client should review the delivered work and communicate any corrections related to the agreed requirements.

Requests that introduce new functionality or a significantly different direction may be treated as additional work.

9. Domain and Hosting

Domain registration, hosting, third-party subscriptions and other external services are separate unless specifically included in the project quotation.

Recurring charges for such services are normally the client's responsibility.

10. Third-Party Services

Projects may use services such as Firebase, hosting providers, APIs, payment services, analytics platforms or other third-party tools when required.

Availability, limitations, pricing or changes made by third-party providers may affect the project independently of the development work.

11. Backend and Database

When a project includes backend or database functionality, the final setup depends on the selected technology, project scope and agreed implementation.

Database hosting, usage limits and third-party service charges may apply separately where applicable.

12. Maintenance

Maintenance refers to updates or corrections within the agreed maintenance scope.

New pages, new functionality, major redesigns or additional modules are not automatically included in maintenance work.

13. Bug Fixes

Bugs related to the agreed development scope can be reviewed and corrected according to the project conditions.

Problems caused by unrelated third-party changes, external services or client-side modifications may require separate work.

14. Project Cancellation

If a project is cancelled after development work has started, the amount already spent on completed work, resources or agreed project activities may not be reversible.

Any cancellation-related arrangement will be discussed according to the stage and actual work completed.

15. No Refund Policy

Payments made for accepted services, development work, maintenance, bug fixing, customisation or other project-related work are non-refundable.

This applies after payment has been made, including where the client later decides not to continue with the project.

16. Developer Fixes and Corrections

The no-refund policy does not prevent the developer from correcting valid issues related to the agreed project scope.

If the delivered website, application or software contains a genuine bug or implementation issue within the agreed requirements, the developer will review and fix the issue according to the applicable project conditions.

A new feature, additional page, redesign, new module, new workflow or functionality outside the agreed scope is not considered a bug fix and may require an additional charge.

17. Client or Third-Party Modifications

Issues caused by modifications made by the client or another developer after delivery may be assessed separately.

Any additional correction required because of such changes may be treated as a separate service.

18. Communication

Project communication should remain clear and focused on the agreed requirements.

Important scope, price or feature changes should be confirmed before implementation.

19. Project Examples

Completed projects may be shown in the Yadav Web Technologies project portfolio unless the project arrangement specifically requires otherwise.

20. Final Project Understanding

The exact deliverables, pricing, features, timeline, maintenance scope and handover details should be confirmed before development begins.

These terms provide the general service structure. Project-specific discussions or quotations may contain additional details relevant to a particular project.
