Client Service Terms

These Terms & Conditions explain the basic terms that apply when purchasing web development services from Yadav Web Technologies.

By purchasing or accepting a web development service from Yadav Web Technologies, you acknowledge that you have reviewed and accepted the following terms.

1. Training & Support

With every purchase of our web development service, you receive 14 days of free training to help you understand how to manage and maintain your website with confidence.

This training offer is valid until 31st September 2026.

2. Pricing & Quotation

Our pricing is not fixed for every project. The final price depends on the specific requirements of the project, including its scope, complexity, features, integrations and development work.

A final quotation can be provided after discussing and understanding the project requirements.

Fixed-price proposals are provided exclusively through our official WhatsApp communication channel so that the agreed requirements and price remain clearly documented.

Please use the contact information available on our Contact page to begin the quotation process.

Any price discussed informally through email, social media or other third-party channels is indicative only and is not considered a final quotation until a formal quote is provided.

3. Maintenance Charges

The included training is intended to help you manage your website independently.

If you later request maintenance or other support directly from Yadav Web Technologies, additional maintenance charges may apply.

Any applicable charge will be communicated and agreed upon before the requested work begins.

4. Delivery Policy

After completion, the agreed project code and deliverables will be tested and reviewed before being handed over to the client.

The delivered work is reviewed for general functionality, performance and implementation according to the agreed project requirements.

Hosting and domain setup are not automatically included in the base development service unless specifically agreed as part of the project.

Any separate hosting, domain or third-party service cost will be communicated before proceeding.

5. Refund Policy

Once the completed code has been delivered and accepted by the client, the payment is considered final and no refund will be issued.

Clients are encouraged to review the agreed project requirements and final deliverables carefully before confirming acceptance.

6. Backend Ownership

Where a project requires a backend service, database or related infrastructure, the preferred setup is made using the client's own account.

This allows the client to retain control over their account, data and backend environment.

Yadav Web Technologies may provide setup guidance and development assistance, but the client's account remains under the client's control.

7. Project Scope & Additional Work

Development is based on the requirements discussed and agreed before the project begins.

Requests that introduce new pages, major features, additional systems, new workflows or substantial changes outside the agreed scope may require additional charges.

Any additional work and its applicable cost will be discussed before the work is undertaken.

8. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing accurate information, required content, images, documents, account access and other materials needed for the project.

Delays caused by missing information, delayed approvals or unavailable access may affect the project timeline.

9. Project Acceptance

A project is considered accepted when the client confirms that the agreed deliverables have been received and are satisfactory according to the discussed requirements.

Clients should review the delivered project and communicate any relevant issues within the agreed review period.

10. Intellectual Property

Ownership of the final delivered project code transfers to the client after full payment has been received.

Yadav Web Technologies may display completed projects in its portfolio, website or case studies for demonstration purposes unless otherwise agreed with the client.

11. Confidentiality

This includes project information, business details, internal processes, credentials and other information that is not intended for public use.

Such external issues may affect the availability or operation of features that depend on those services.

14. Force Majeure

We will not be responsible for delays caused by circumstances outside our reasonable control, including natural disasters, major internet interruptions, infrastructure failures or government actions.

15. Updates to These Terms

These Client Service Terms may be updated when necessary. Updated terms will be published on the website.

The terms applicable to a project will generally be based on the terms communicated and accepted at the time of the service arrangement, subject to any separately agreed changes.

If you have questions regarding these Client Service Terms, project pricing, service scope or any other development-related matter, please contact us before purchasing or starting the requested service.
