Client Services

Client Service Terms

These terms describe the general conditions that may apply when engaging Yadav Web Technologies for website, web application, custom software or digital development work.

Service Terms

Project-specific work is based on an agreed scope.

These general service terms are intended to provide a clear understanding of how project requirements, communication, revisions, delivery and related responsibilities may be handled.

1. Project Scope

The project scope is based on the requirements discussed and agreed for the particular project. Features, pages, modules and integrations outside the agreed scope may require separate discussion.

2. Requirement Confirmation

Clients are expected to provide sufficiently clear information about the intended purpose, required features, content and workflow of the project.

3. Project Pricing

Pricing depends on the agreed scope, functionality, design requirements, integrations, technical complexity and other project-specific factors.

4. Payment Arrangement

Where a project requires an advance, milestone payment or other payment arrangement, the applicable terms should be agreed before development begins.

5. Client Content

Clients are responsible for providing accurate text, images, logos, documents and other materials required for the project, and for having the right to provide those materials.

6. Design & Development

Development is carried out according to the agreed project requirements and technical approach. Certain implementation details may be adjusted where reasonably necessary for functionality, compatibility or usability.

7. Revisions

Revisions should remain related to the agreed project scope. Major changes to the approved requirements, structure or functionality may be treated as additional scope.

8. Additional Features

New modules, pages, integrations or substantial changes requested after scope confirmation may require additional development work and a revised project arrangement.

9. Client Communication

Timely communication, feedback and approval from the client help avoid unnecessary delays in the development process.

10. Delivery

Project delivery is based on the agreed scope, availability of required client materials, communication and completion of applicable project obligations.

11. Third-Party Services

Projects may use third-party services such as hosting, domains, Firebase, APIs, payment services or other platforms. Their availability, pricing, policies and technical limitations may be outside the direct control of the developer.

12. Hosting & Domain

Hosting, domain registration, renewal and related third-party charges may be separate from development charges unless specifically included in the project arrangement.

13. Deployment

Deployment may involve configuration of hosting, repositories, APIs, databases or other services. Final deployment requirements depend on the selected technical environment.

14. Maintenance & Future Changes

Future updates, maintenance, new features, redesigns, content changes or additional modules may be discussed separately after project delivery.

15. Intellectual Property

Ownership and permitted use of project-specific code, design, content and other materials should follow the arrangement agreed for the particular project. Third-party components remain subject to their respective licenses.

16. Security & Credentials

Where clients provide passwords, API keys, hosting access or other credentials, they should use appropriate security practices and avoid sharing sensitive credentials through insecure channels.

17. Third-Party Content

Clients are responsible for ensuring that supplied text, images, fonts, logos, documents and other content do not unlawfully infringe third-party rights.

18. Delays

Project schedules can be affected by delayed feedback, missing content, unavailable services, changes in requirements, technical dependencies or other circumstances outside reasonable control.

19. Project Cancellation

If a project is cancelled or substantially changed after work has started, the treatment of completed work, payments and deliverables should be determined according to the agreed project arrangement and applicable circumstances.

20. Acceptance

Where a project is delivered for review, the client should communicate any relevant scope-related issues within the agreed review process so that they can be addressed appropriately.

Important

Project agreements may contain additional terms.

These Client Service Terms provide general guidance. A specific project may have additional written requirements, pricing conditions, delivery milestones, revision limits, maintenance arrangements or other terms agreed between the parties.
